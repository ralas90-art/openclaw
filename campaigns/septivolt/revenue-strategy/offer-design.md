# SeptiVolt Founding Partner Pilot Offer Strategy

This document presents the strategic offer design, sales positioning, and closing script for the **SeptiVolt Founding Partner Pilot** campaign.

---

## 1. Offer Name
**SeptiVolt Founding Partner Pilot** (Early-Access Cohort)

## 2. Target Buyer
Solar Owners, EPC Leaders, and Sales Managers who are responsible for training, ramping, and scaling solar sales divisions.

## 3. Core Pain
- **Slow Rep Ramp-up:** New solar reps take 60–90 days to close their first deal, draining cash flow.
- **High Rep Churn:** High turnover of new hires who quit due to lack of confidence and early rejection.
- **Management Shadowing Overhead:** Managers waste hours shadowing reps in the field or listening to recorded calls.
- **Wasted Leads:** Inexperienced reps burn through expensive marketing leads while practicing pitches live.

## 4. Current Broken Alternative
- **Passive Video Training:** Reps click through slide decks without active retention.
- **Unrealistic peer roleplays:** Peer roleplays lack realistic objection pushback or objective scoring.
- **"Trial by Fire":** Sending reps to the doors or phones immediately, burning high-cost opportunities.

## 5. SeptiVolt Positioning
- **The AI Solar Sales Training OS:** Built specifically for solar sales teams.
- **Realistic practice environment:** Interactive simulation modules designed to help reps practice objection handling (D2D and virtual).
- **Manager visibility:** Supports faster ramp-up and helps managers see training progress via automated scoring and feedback dashboards.

## 6. Founding Partner Pilot Structure
- **Cohort Capacity:** Limited to 5 progressive solar companies/EPCs.
- **Onboarding Alignment:** Hands-on setup where SeptiVolt engineers construct custom avatars mimicking local objections.

## 7. What the Client Gets (The Value Stack)
- **10 active rep seats:** Full access to the SeptiVolt AI simulator.
- **3 custom objection avatars:** Objection scenarios built for their specific territory (e.g., D2D, telesales, virtual).
- **Automated Manager Dashboard:** High-fidelity grade sheets and script adherence tracking.
- **Custom Playbook Integration:** Uploading company-specific playbooks and custom scripts.
- **Private support channel:** Slack access directly to SeptiVolt developers.

## 8. Pilot Duration
**60 Days** of structured sandbox testing and team training.

## 9. Recommended Pricing Options
- **Flat-Fee Option:** $1,500 flat fee for the full 60 days (regularly $1,500/month + $2,500 setup fee).
- **Performance-Aligned Option:** $500 setup fee, then $150 per rep certified as "Field Ready" through the simulator.

## 10. Founding Partner Bonuses
- **Bonus 1:** Lifetime Price Lock (50% discount on monthly licensing post-pilot).
- **Bonus 2:** Avatar Expansion Pack (2 additional custom objection personas).
- **Bonus 3:** Rep script compliance audit.

## 11. Risk Reversal (The Guarantee)
**100% Ramp-Up Guarantee:** If your reps complete at least 50 simulated sales conversations, and you do not feel the platform has supported faster ramp-up, receive a full refund of your pilot fee.

## 12. Scarcity/Urgency Angle
To maintain premium onboarding support and custom objection avatar setups, this pilot is strictly capped at **5 solar companies**.

## 13. Sales Call CTA
Book a SeptiVolt Demo (Virtual 20-Minute Sandbox Session)
