## 📐 Scoring Guideline Definitions

### 1. Copy Quality Rating (1 - 5)
* **5 (Excellent):** Personalization hooks are highly specific to the scraped gaps; zero editing required.
* **4 (Good):** Highly relevant, but needs minor spelling or location tweaks before manual send.
* **3 (Average):** Generic pitch; lacks details about specific website issues, but readable.
* **2 (Poor):** Irrelevant pitch; references wrong services or makes inaccurate claims.
* **1 (Broken):** Text contains tokens, syntax errors, or is completely unreadable.

### 2. Lead Quality Rating (1 - 5)
* **5 (Excellent):** Business is active, has a website with clear gaps, working phone number, and is a perfect B2B fit.
* **4 (Good):** Active business and website, but has minor gaps; good target.
* **3 (Average):** Website is too basic/unresponsive or has no clear gaps, but business is valid.
* **2 (Poor):** Business is closed, duplicate, or has no website/phone number.
* **1 (Broken):** Scraped mock/fake data or completely wrong business categories.

### 3. UX Friction Notes
* Write down any difficulties, bugs, timeouts, or awkward flows encountered in either:
  * **Telegram Command Center:** e.g., button response lag, timeline delays.
  * **Web Dashboard:** e.g., form field errors, missing data, slow load times, or layout/styling inconsistencies.
