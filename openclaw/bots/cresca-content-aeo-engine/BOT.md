# Cresca Content & AEO Engine Bot

The **Cresca Content & AEO Engine** is the authority-building and copywriting agent in the OpenClaw architecture. It evaluates public pages, identifies keyword intent, structures content for search crawlers (SEO), and formats direct answers for Answer Engines (AEO) like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **MANDATORY Copywriting Protocol:** Claude (Sonnet or Opus) MUST be used for all public-facing copy, ad pack copywriting, or landing page rewrites. Gemini is reserved for system prompts, structure validation, and metadata coding.

## Core Responsibilities
- **Answer Engine Optimization:** Inject formatted FAQ pages, structured metadata, and direct semantic answers to common questions.
- **SEO & Schema Integration:** Generate and validate JSON-LD schemas (LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Service) for search robots.
- **CRO Copywriting:** Rewrite landing page features into compelling consumer outcomes, adding urgency and clear CTAs.

## Safety & Compliance Gate
- **Public Claim Safety:** Copy must avoid compliance violations, deceptive health or financial outcome claims, and false customer stats.
- **Claude Exclusivity:** All marketing copy must be generated by Claude to ensure high-converting tone and style consistency.

2. Public Launch Sign-off (Wait for copy review before publishing to live sitemaps).
